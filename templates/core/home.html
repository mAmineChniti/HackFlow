// Why HackFlow

Everything you need to go from idea to demo day.

Hackathons, challenges, teams and judging — one platform instead of six spreadsheets and a very tired Discord server.

For hackers + organizers
01

Launch in minutes

Spin up a branded hackathon page, open registration and start collecting teams before your coffee gets cold.

02

Challenges that ship

Always-on CTF, build and AI challenges with automated tests, clear scoring and write-ups when the dust settles.

03

Live leaderboards

Real-time rankings, transparent point breakdowns and anti-cheat signals so the top of the board actually means something.

04

Teams that click

Go solo or match with designers, engineers and storytellers by skills, timezone and what you actually want to build.

05

Judging you can trust

Rubric-based scoring, judge assignments and conflict-of-interest checks. Published criteria, zero mystery winners.

Insights that matter

Participation, retention, submission quality and sponsor ROI — the numbers you need to prove the event worked.
